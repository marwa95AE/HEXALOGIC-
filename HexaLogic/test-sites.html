How the score is calculated

Each site receives a manually assigned score from 1 (low match) to 5 (strong match) for each criterion. The weighted result is out of 100:

Similarity = Σ (criterion score × criterion weight) ÷ 5

This beginner-friendly comparison is an educational estimate, not a scientific measurement. Earth sites do not reproduce the Moon's vacuum or low gravity, or the full conditions on Mars.
